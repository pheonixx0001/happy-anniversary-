My Love,

Six months with you, and somehow, six months still feels too small for everything you mean to me. There is something incredibly beautiful about the way you have become such a natural part of my life, as if your place in it was always meant to be there.

I love the quiet things about loving you. I love having someone whose name can make an ordinary moment feel warmer. Someone I want to tell the smallest things to. Someone whose happiness matters to me simply because it is yours.

You have this beautiful way of making life feel softer without even trying. Your presence has become one of those little things I never want to take for granted, because somewhere along the way, you became much more than just someone I love — you became someone my heart naturally looks for.

If I had to describe what you feel like to me, I think I would call it home. Not a place, not four walls, but that quiet feeling of comfort you get when you know there is someone in this world who means something incredibly special to you.

I hope you always know how deeply you are loved. I hope you know how beautiful your presence is, how much your little ways matter, and how easily you can turn a normal moment into something I want to remember.

I don't think love needs the biggest words in the world. Sometimes it is simply choosing to care, choosing to stay close, choosing to appreciate someone, and quietly being grateful that out of everyone in this world, you found each other.

And if I could keep only one thought from these six months, it would be this: I am really, really glad that it is you.

Happy six months, my love. Six months of having you in my world, and somehow I still feel lucky every time I get to call you mine. ❤️

I love you — in the quiet moments, in the silly moments, in the little moments that nobody else sees, and in all the ways words will probably never be enough to explain.

yours, with all my love ♡
