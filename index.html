For You, Ananta

SIX MONTHS · FROM MY HEART

My Love,

Six months sounds like a simple number, but when I look at it and realise that every day of those six months somehow carries a little piece of you, it feels like something much bigger. It feels like a whole little world quietly growing between two hearts — made of your name, your smile, your presence, and all the beautiful feelings that words never seem to hold quite perfectly.

You have become one of the softest thoughts in my mind and one of the sweetest parts of my days. There is something incredibly special about knowing that somewhere in this enormous world, there is a person whose name can instantly make an ordinary moment feel warmer. Sometimes it is not even a big thing. Sometimes it is simply seeing your name, remembering your smile, or thinking about you for no particular reason — and suddenly the day feels a little prettier.

I love the little things that make you unmistakably you. The way you care, the way you love, the little details in your personality, the softness you carry without even trying. I do not want some perfect, polished version of you. I love the real you — the girl with her own little habits, expressions, moods, dreams and beautiful imperfections that make you impossible to replace.

If I could give you one feeling and make sure you carried it with you everywhere, it would be the feeling of being completely cherished. I would want you to know that you deserve gentle words, warm mornings, pretty flowers, silly smiles, peaceful moments and all the tiny beautiful things that make life feel worth pausing for. Most of all, I would want you to know that you never have to become someone else to be worthy of love. You are already enough simply by being you.

I think some of the most beautiful parts of love are the quiet ones. It is wanting to tell someone the most random thought. It is seeing something pretty and immediately wishing they could see it too. It is remembering tiny details because they matter when they belong to someone you care about. And somehow, you became that person for me — the person my mind naturally turns towards when something feels beautiful, funny, exciting or simply worth sharing.

There is a place in my heart that feels beautifully yours. I did not plan it, and I did not have to force it. It just happened, quietly and naturally, until one day I realised how much of my world had started carrying little traces of you. And honestly, I would not change that for anything.

So on our six months, I do not want to make this complicated. I just want you to know that I am grateful for you, proud to call you someone so precious to me, and genuinely happy that out of all the people in this enormous world, somehow our paths found each other.

And if this entire page had to disappear tomorrow and leave behind only one sentence, I would want that sentence to be this: I love you, Ananta. Quietly, deeply, sincerely — in all the little ways that make love feel real.
